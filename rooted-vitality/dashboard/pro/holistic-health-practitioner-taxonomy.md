# Holistic Health Practitioner Taxonomy
## Comprehensive Analysis for Rooted Vitality Platform

**Research Date:** October 31, 2025  
**Purpose:** Complete taxonomy for practitioner-client matching system  
**Scope:** US market only (state licensing requirements noted for future research)

---

## Executive Summary

This document provides a comprehensive taxonomy of holistic health practitioner categories for the Rooted Vitality platform. The research identified **22 major practitioner categories** with detailed subcategories (checkboxes) for each, organized by credential requirements. This taxonomy is designed to support a two-way matching system where clients select needs and practitioners select offerings using the same checkbox structure.

### Key Findings:
- **Licensing varies dramatically by state** - some categories (massage, acupuncture) heavily regulated, others (health coaching, energy healing) largely unregulated
- **Thumbtack and HomeAdvisor** have tested these categories extensively with billions in investment
- **~350+ total modalities** exist across all categories, but can be organized into **20-25 major categories** with **10-40 subcategories each**
- **Three-tier credential system:** Licensed (state-required) → Certified (industry-standard) → No Formal Requirement

## PART 2: COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

### THUMBTACK STRUCTURE
From analysis of Thumbtack's holistic health categories, they organize services into:

**Main Categories:**
- Holistic Health Services (umbrella)
- Health Coaches
- Nutritionists/Dieticians
- Holistic Medicine Practitioners
- Massage Therapy
- Alternative Healing

**Strengths:**
- Well-tested taxonomy with billions in investment
- Clear separation between licensed and non-licensed practitioners
- Strong SEO and search functionality
- Good filtering by specialty

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited two-way search (practitioners can't search for clients)
- Generic category names don't capture full specialization
- Poor differentiation between similar modalities
- Limited practitioner profile customization

### HOMEADVISOR STRUCTURE
HomeAdvisor focuses primarily on home services and has limited holistic health offerings. Their wellness categories are minimal:
- Home health care services
- Wellness-related home modifications
- Not a strong competitor in holistic health space

**Opportunity:** HomeAdvisor has NOT penetrated this market effectively, leaving massive opportunity for Rooted Vitality

### KEY DIFFERENTIATORS FOR ROOTED VITALITY:
1. **Two-way search** - practitioners can proactively find clients
2. **Exact checkbox matching** - no partial matches cluttering results
3. **"Open to contact" flag** - clients can opt into practitioner outreach
4. **More granular specialization** - deeper subcategories than Thumbtack
5. **Credential transparency** - clear licensing/certification display

### DATABASE STRUCTURE RECOMMENDATIONS:

**Practitioners Table:**
```
- practitioner_id
- primary_category (dropdown from 22 categories)
- checkboxes (array/JSON of selected offerings)
- credentials (license #, certifications)
- credential_verification (verified badge)
- service_area (location/radius)
- pricing_structure
- availability
```

**Projects Table:**
```
- project_id
- client_id
- category (dropdown from 22 categories)
- checkboxes (array/JSON of needs)
- status (open/matched/completed)
- open_to_contact (boolean flag)
- budget_range
```

**Matching Algorithm:**
```sql
-- Exact checkbox matching
SELECT practitioners 
WHERE category = project.category 
AND practitioner.checkboxes @> project.checkboxes
-- (PostgreSQL array containment operator)
```

### CHECKBOX TAXONOMY PRINCIPLES:
1. **Condition-based** (what client needs treatment for)
2. **Modality-based** (specific techniques practitioner offers)
3. **Population-based** (demographics: seniors, prenatal, children, athletes)
4. **Mutual exclusivity** (minimal overlap between checkboxes)
5. **Plain language** (no jargon - client-friendly terms)
6. **Scalable** (can add new checkboxes without breaking system)

## PART 6: REGULATORY CONSIDERATIONS

### KEY COMPLIANCE ISSUES:
1. **Scope of Practice** - practitioners must clearly state they cannot diagnose/treat/cure diseases (unless licensed to do so)
2. **Credential Verification** - platform should verify licenses for regulated categories
3. **Insurance Coverage** - track which services may be insurance-reimbursable
4. **HIPAA Compliance** - if practitioners handle health info through platform
5. **Payment Processing** - especially for services that may be reimbursable
6. **State-Specific Regulations** - some states restrict certain terminology (e.g., "doctor," "physician")

### RECOMMENDED DISCLAIMERS:
- Clear scope of practice statements for each category
- Credential verification badges for licensed practitioners
- Client agreements acknowledging services are not medical treatment (for non-licensed categories)
- Platform not liable for practitioner claims/outcomes (standard marketplace disclaimer)
