What is a Wellness Journey?

A wellness journey is your personal health initiative. Whether you're managing a health goal, undergoing treatment, or exploring a wellness path, journeys help you organize your path and connect with the right practitioners.

Finding Practitioners

Once you create a wellness journey, you can browse practitioners matched to your specific needs. Review their credentials, ratings, and approach before connecting.
