Why Pricing Matters for Your Practice

Underpricing your services can harm your practice and signal low confidence in your expertise. Overpricing may limit your client base and lead to fewer bookings. Strategic pricing finds the sweet spotâ€��maximizing your income while remaining competitive and accessible to your ideal clients.

On Rooted Vitality, your pricing sends a message to potential clients about your qualifications and the quality of care you provide. Clear, transparent pricing also builds trust and reduces friction during the booking process.

How to Set Your Rate Card

Research your market. Look at rates in your geographic area and within your practice specialty. Consider:

  • Your level of certification and training
  • Years of practice experience
  • Local cost of living and competition
  • Demand for your services in your region

Define your offerings. Break down your services into clear packages or session types:

  • Initial consultation (often shorter, foundational)
  • Standard session (your core offering)
  • Extended session (deeper work or specialty)
  • Package deals (e.g., 5 or 10 sessions at a discounted rate)

Calculate your costs. Factor in:

  • Your time and expertise
  • Materials or supplies used
  • Overhead (space, equipment, insurance)
  • Taxes and business expenses
  • Marketing and client acquisition

Pricing Models: From Hourly to Value-Based

Hourly rates are simple and transparent. Most practitioners start here. A 1-hour session might be $75â€��$200+, depending on your specialty and market.

Per-session rates (flat fee) work well for standardized offerings. This removes the clock-watching pressure and lets you focus on the client's outcome.

Package pricing encourages commitment and creates steady revenue. Offering a 5-session package at 10â€��15% off incentivizes clients to book ongoing care.

Value-based pricing ties your fee to the outcome or transformation you deliver. For example, "Energy alignment sessions: $120" or "Nutritional consultation package: $300." This reflects the real value clients receive.

Sliding scale (optional) allows you to serve clients with lower incomes while maintaining your base rate for others. Be consistent and transparent about your sliding scale policy.

Raising Your Rates Strategically

As you gain experience, build client reviews, and increase demand, your rates should increase too. Here's how to do it smoothly:

Plan ahead. Decide on a rate increase 2â€��3 months before implementing it.

Grandfather existing clients. Honor current rates for loyal clients for a set period (e.g., 6 months), then transition them to new pricing. This builds goodwill.

Communicate clearly. Explain your rate increase in a personal message: "As I expand my training and client base, I'm adjusting rates to reflect my growing expertise. I'm grateful for your continued support."

Raise rates gradually. Increase by 10â€��15% every 1â€��2 years, rather than doubling overnight.

Closing: Pricing Is Self-Care for Your Practice

Confident pricing reflects your confidence in your work. You deserve to be compensated fairly for your expertise and time. By setting clear, strategic rates and communicating them with pride, you attract clients who value your services and are committed to their healing journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's a good starting rate for a new practitioner?

Research rates in your area and specialty. New practitioners often start 10-20% below experienced practitioners. As you build reviews and expertise, raise your rates. Remember: underpricing signals low confidence.

Should I offer different rates for different clients?

You can offer a sliding scale for clients with lower incomes, but be consistent and transparent about it. Don't vary rates arbitrarilyâ€��it creates mistrust and legal complications.

How often should I raise my rates?

Plan rate increases every 1-2 years as you gain experience and build reputation. A 10-15% increase is typical. Always communicate changes clearly to existing clients.

Should I offer package discounts?

Yes. Package discounts (e.g., 10-15% off for 5-session bundles) encourage client commitment and create steady revenue. They also increase your bookings.
