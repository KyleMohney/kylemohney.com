What is Pay-Per-Lead?

Pay-per-lead (PPL) is a performance-based business model where you earn money for each qualified client lead you receive. Unlike traditional hourly rates or project-based fees, you only pay when a genuine, actionable potential client comes your way.

How It Works with Rooted Vitality

  1. Profile Creation: Set up your professional profile with your expertise, services, and availability.
  2. Lead Distribution: We match qualified clients seeking your specific services with your practice.
  3. Lead Delivery: You receive pre-vetted leads via our platform.
  4. Conversion & Engagement: You contact the lead, discuss services, and potentially book a consultation.
  5. Payment: You're charged a flat fee per lead, regardless of whether the lead converts to a paid client.

Key Advantages

Predictable Cost Structure

You know exactly what each lead costs upfront. There are no surprises or variable expenses tied to conversion rates.

Scalable Growth

As your practice grows and your capacity increases, you can request more leads. Scale at your own pace.

No Marketing Overhead

Skip expensive advertising and marketing campaigns. We handle client acquisition so you can focus on delivery.

Steady Client Pipeline

Receive consistent, qualified leads instead of relying on word-of-mouth or unpredictable referrals.

Who Benefits Most from PPL?

Pay-per-lead works best for practitioners who:

  • Have strong communication and follow-up skills
  • Want to minimize marketing costs
  • Are looking to grow their client base quickly
  • Have availability to work with new clients consistently
  • Are confident in their ability to convert interested leads
Pro Tip: The quality of your profile and your follow-up responsiveness directly impact your ROI. Invest time in both, and the pay-per-lead model becomes one of your most profitable growth channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a lead cost?

Lead costs vary based on your specialty, location, and service type. Contact Rooted Vitality for current pricing specific to your practice. Most practitioners find the cost significantly lower than traditional advertising.

What if a lead doesn't respond or convert?

You still pay for the lead since we delivered a qualified inquiry. However, strong follow-up practices typically achieve 40-60% conversion rates. Focus on improving your response time and communication to maximize conversions.

Can I pause or adjust my lead volume?

Yes! You control how many leads you receive. If you're fully booked, pause new leads. When you have availability, increase your volume. This flexibility is a key advantage of the PPL model.

How are leads qualified before delivery?

Rooted Vitality screens leads to ensure they match your service area, specialty, and availability. We filter out spam, duplicates, and unqualified inquiries so you receive only genuine potential clients.

Is pay-per-lead better than traditional advertising?

For most practitioners, yes. Traditional ads require upfront investment with no guarantee of results. With PPL, you only pay when you receive a qualified lead, making it lower risk and more predictable.
