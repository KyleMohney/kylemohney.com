Your Practice Dashboard

Rooted Vitality gives you a complete suite of tools to manage your practice. Your dashboard is your command center—everything you need is organized in one place so you can focus on what matters: meaningful connections with clients.

What You'll Find

Your practice area includes your Clients page where all match requests appear, your Inbox for direct messaging with clients, and your Settings where you control everything about how you work. Each area is designed to make managing your practice simple and intuitive. Whether you're reviewing a new match, communicating with a client, or adjusting your availability, everything is just a few clicks away.

Match Settings & Customization

Your Match Settings are where you define how you work on Rooted Vitality. This is where you have complete control over your practice.

Control Every Detail

Through Match Settings, you can manage your Profile (how clients see you), your Specialties (what you offer), your Travel Coverage (where and how you work), and your Match Status (whether you're available to receive new matches). Each setting can be adjusted independently, so you can pause one specialty, adjust your coverage area, or turn matching on and off—all without affecting the rest of your setup. Your practice evolves as you do.

Stay Organized

Your Inbox keeps all client conversations in one place. New matches appear in your new messages tab, and you can access client information, wellness profiles, and contact details directly from the conversation thread. Everything you need to communicate with a client is right there.

Your Profile: Your First Impression

Your practitioner profile is how clients get to know you. You can update your background, credentials, approach, specialties, and add a personal introduction video from your settings. Your profile completeness affects your visibility in the directory—the more complete your profile, the higher you appear in search results. We suggest keeping your profile fresh and updated as your practice evolves.

Tools & Integrations

Rooted Vitality works with the tools you already use. We've integrated with popular CRM platforms so you can sync your client data seamlessly. If you use HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, HighLevel, ServiceTitan, mHelpDesk, or Zoho CRM, you can connect it directly from your Settings.

Extend Your Practice

You have flexibility in how you work. Connect integrations that support your workflow, adjust your settings to match your availability, and use the platform features that work best for you. The tools are here to serve your practice, not the other way around.
