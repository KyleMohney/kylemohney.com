How Rooted Vitality Works: Membership-Based Matchmaking

Rooted Vitality operates on a membership model designed to create meaningful connections between practitioners and clients. With a monthly membership, you gain access to unlimited match potential. There's no cap on leads, no ceiling on opportunity—just a direct pathway to clients actively seeking the care you offer.

The Model

Clients browse the directory to find practitioners whose specialties, services, and availability match exactly what they're looking for. When they find someone who feels right, they send a match request. From there, it's up to you and the client to have a genuine conversation and determine if working together makes sense. This is how real partnerships form—through mutual interest and authentic connection.

Your Complete Control

You define everything about how you work on Rooted Vitality. This is your practice, your terms, your vision.

Define Your Offering

You choose what services you offer, how much you charge, what geographic areas you cover, and whether you practice in-person, virtually, or both. You control your specialties, your availability, your pricing—all of it. And if things change? You can update your settings anytime. Turn your matching on or off as needed. Your practice evolves with you.

Direct Relationships, Fair Partnerships

All interactions happen directly between you and clients through our platform. All payments flow directly between you—we don't take a percentage or commission. We're here to connect you, not to profit from your work. When you build a strong practice on Rooted Vitality, you keep 100% of what you earn.

Meaningful Connections

At Rooted Vitality, we believe in the power of holistic wellness. We believe that real healing happens when the right practitioner meets the right client. Our mission is to make those meaningful connections possible.

Why This Matters

Clients come to Rooted Vitality because they want to find genuine practitioners who are aligned with their wellness journey. Practitioners join us because they want to connect with clients who truly value their expertise and approach. The membership model allows us to match you with people actively seeking what you offer, without artificial limits or per-lead fees. You get unlimited potential. Clients get real choice. Everyone benefits from honest, fair partnerships built on genuine connection.
