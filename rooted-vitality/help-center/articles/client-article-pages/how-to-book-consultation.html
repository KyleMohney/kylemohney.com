Step 1: Create Your Care Request

Begin on your My Wellness page by creating a new care request. This is where you share what you're looking for and what matters most to you:

  • Wellness Category: Choose the primary focus—acupuncture, herbalism, coaching, or another modality
  • Subcategories: Select specific areas within that category that match your needs
  • Travel Preferences: Let us know if you prefer in-person, virtual, or either
  • Level of Interest: Indicate your openness to practitioners—are you looking for someone established or open to newer practitioners?

The more thoughtfully you share about what matters to you, the better we can connect you with practitioners who truly align with your needs.

Step 2: Explore Your Matches

Once you submit your care request, you'll see a curated list of practitioners whose approach resonates with what you're looking for. Take your time reviewing their profiles—each one has been matched because they align with your preferences and what you shared.

Feel free to connect with multiple practitioners. When you match with someone, your request enters a pending state in your inbox, and they'll receive your request to review.

Step 3: Connect and Decide

Once a practitioner accepts your request, you can reach out directly. Use our instant messenger or phone to introduce yourself, learn more about their approach, and find a time for your first consultation that works for both of you.

After your conversation or initial session, you'll know whether this feels like the right partnership. You can match with multiple practitioners, but you'll choose one per care request to work with as your primary guide.

When you're ready, mark them as Hired to move forward together, or Not Hired if you'd like to explore other options. This helps keep everything organized and lets practitioners know your decision.
