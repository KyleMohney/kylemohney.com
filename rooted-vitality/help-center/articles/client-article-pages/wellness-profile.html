What is Your Wellness Profile?

Your Wellness Profile is an optional questionnaire that helps practitioners better understand your health background and wellness needs. It's a space for you to share relevant information about your current health, lifestyle, and wellness goals.

You can complete your wellness profile when creating a new care request, or anytime from your account profile. It's entirely optional—but completing it helps practitioners provide more informed and personalized support for your wellness journey.

How Your Profile is Used

Shared with Matched Practitioners Only

Your wellness profile information is only shared with practitioners who have accepted your care request match. This means:

  • Practitioners you haven't matched with cannot see your profile
  • Your information remains private until a match is established and accepted
  • You have control over who sees your wellness information

Not Used for Matching

Your wellness profile answers are not used to match you with practitioners. Instead, matching is based on your care request preferences—such as your wellness focus, specific categories, and location preferences. Your wellness profile is only shared after a match has been made and accepted.

Not Medical Advice

Rooted Vitality does not use your wellness profile to make medical recommendations or diagnoses. All health guidance and recommendations come directly from your matched practitioners during your interactions. Remember: all interactions are between you and your practitioner, and they are responsible for ensuring safe, appropriate care.

Helps Practitioners Assist You Better

By sharing relevant wellness information, your matched practitioners can:

  • Better understand your health background and current situation
  • Tailor their approach to your specific needs and lifestyle
  • Ask more informed follow-up questions during consultations
  • Provide more personalized wellness recommendations

Completing Your Profile

What Information to Share

Your wellness profile may ask about:

  • Your current health status and any existing health conditions
  • Medications, supplements, or treatments you're currently using
  • Your lifestyle habits (sleep, exercise, diet, stress levels)
  • Past health experiences or wellness history
  • Your wellness goals and what you hope to achieve
  • Any allergies or sensitivities
  • Areas of discomfort or concern

Be Honest and Thorough

The more accurate and detailed your wellness profile, the better your matched practitioners can understand your situation and provide appropriate support. There's no judgment—practitioners see clients with diverse health backgrounds every day.

You Can Always Update It

Your wellness profile isn't permanent. You can update or edit your answers anytime from your account profile. If your health situation changes or you want to add more information, simply update your profile before or after sharing it with a practitioner.

Privacy and Control

Your Information is Protected

Your wellness profile information is treated with care and confidentiality. It's only visible to practitioners you've intentionally matched with who have accepted your care request.

Optional, Not Required

Completing your wellness profile is completely optional. You can create care requests and work with practitioners without filling it out. However, we encourage you to complete it when possible, as it helps practitioners provide better support.

You're in Control

Remember, all interactions on Rooted Vitality are between you and your practitioner. You decide what information to share, with whom, and when. If you're uncomfortable sharing certain information with a practitioner, you're not obligated to do so.
