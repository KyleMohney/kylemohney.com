Reviews Policy

Effective Date: December 1, 2025

Rooted Vitality believes in the power of authentic client feedback. This Reviews Policy explains how our review system works and the guidelines that help maintain a respectful, honest community where all voices are heard and valued.

1. Our Review Philosophy

1.1 Reviews as Authentic Expression

At Rooted Vitality, we believe that:

  • Client reviews are authentic expressions of personal opinion and experience
  • We are not the arbiters of truth in reviews
  • Reviews reflect individual perspectives and may vary from person to person
  • Both positive and negative feedback is valuable for our community
  • Practitioners and clients deserve to share their genuine experiences

1.2 Our Role

Rooted Vitality's role is to:

  • Provide a platform for honest client feedback
  • Enforce our review guidelines to keep reviews respectful and safe
  • Remove only reviews that violate our guidelines (not because we disagree with them)
  • Trust clients to evaluate practitioners based on their own values and needs
  • Support both practitioners and clients in a fair, balanced way

2. How Reviews Work

2.1 Posting a Review

You can post a review after you have received services from a practitioner:

  • Reviews can include a star rating (1-5 stars) and written feedback
  • Reviews are optional—posting a review is entirely up to you
  • Your review appears on the practitioner's profile once posted
  • Other clients can see your review to help them make informed decisions

2.2 Editing Your Review

You can edit your review to update or clarify your feedback:

  • Access your review through your account or the practitioner's profile
  • Update your rating or written feedback as you see fit
  • Your edited review will reflect the changes immediately
  • You can modify your review whenever you wish

2.3 Review Permanence

Reviews are permanent once posted:

  • Once you post a review, it becomes part of the platform community
  • You cannot delete or remove your own review
  • You can only edit your review to update its content
  • Reviews remain publicly visible unless they violate our guidelines
  • Your review is a permanent contribution to the community's feedback

2.4 Review Visibility and Control

Reviews are publicly visible and permanent:

  • Reviews are visible on practitioner profiles to all users
  • Visible to anyone accessing the platform (not limited to registered users)
  • Associated with your account (others can see you posted the review)
  • Part of the practitioner's overall rating and reputation
  • You have no control over whether your review stays public once posted
  • Your review remains permanently public unless it violates our review guidelines
  • Rooted Vitality hosts reviews as a platform for community feedback and is not responsible for the content of individual reviews

3. Review Guidelines

3.1 What We Don't Allow in Reviews

Reviews must NOT:

  • Promote violence or harm: Include threats, calls for violence, or encouragement of harmful behavior
  • Promote discrimination: Include hateful content based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics
  • Promote illegal activity: Encourage or describe illegal behavior
  • Use vulgar or blacklisted terminology: Include profanity or offensive language
  • Reveal private information: Share practitioner's personal contact info, address, or other private details
  • Be spam or ads: Promote other businesses or services unrelated to the practitioner's services
  • Be manipulation or fraud: Include false information intended to mislead other users

3.2 What You CAN Include in Reviews

We encourage reviews that include:

  • Your honest opinion about the service you received
  • Specific details about your experience (what worked, what didn't)
  • How the service affected you or your wellness journey
  • Whether you felt the service was worth the price
  • Whether you would recommend this practitioner to others
  • Constructive feedback about how the practitioner could improve
  • Comparison to other practitioners or services you've tried

3.3 Review Content Standards

Reviews should:

  • Be based on your actual experience with the practitioner
  • Be truthful and honest, as you experienced it
  • Be respectful even if critical
  • Focus on the service received, not personal attacks
  • Be clear and understandable to other readers

4. What Constitutes a Review Violation

4.1 Examples of Violations

The following types of reviews will be removed:

  • Violence: "They should be locked up" or threats against the practitioner
  • Hate speech: Slurs or discriminatory language based on protected characteristics
  • Illegal promotion: "I got drugs from this person" or similar illegal content
  • Vulgar language: Excessive profanity or offensive terms
  • Private information: Sharing the practitioner's address, phone number, or email (if not public)
  • Spam: "Check out my competing business" or unrelated promotions
  • Lies: Fabricated stories clearly intended to harm the practitioner's reputation

4.2 Examples of Allowed Negative Reviews

These reviews are NOT violations and will remain:

  • "This service didn't help my situation. I didn't feel a difference."
  • "The practitioner was nice, but their method didn't work for me personally."
  • "I paid $100 for an hour session and didn't get what I expected."
  • "This wasn't for me. I found a different practitioner I like better."
  • "The session felt rushed. I didn't have time to fully explain my concerns."
  • "The practitioner was rude and dismissive of my questions."
  • "I would not recommend this person based on my experience."

5. Our Review Moderation Process

5.1 How We Review Reviews

We use a combination of automated and manual review:

  • Automated Detection: Our system flags reviews that may violate guidelines
  • Manual Review: Our team reviews flagged content for final determination
  • Context Matters: We consider context when evaluating reviews (e.g., mild profanity vs. hate speech)
  • Benefit of the Doubt: When borderline, we lean toward keeping reviews to preserve free expression

5.2 Removal and Appeals

If a review is removed:

  • You will be notified with a reason for the removal
  • You can appeal the removal if you believe it was a mistake
  • Our team will re-evaluate the review and respond to your appeal
  • We aim to process appeals within 5-7 business days

5.3 What We WON'T Do

We will NOT remove reviews because:

  • The practitioner disagrees with the opinion expressed
  • A review is negative or critical
  • A review hurts a practitioner's feelings
  • A review describes a bad experience (even if the practitioner denies it)
  • We think a review is unfair (it's your opinion to share)
  • A practitioner requests we remove a review
  • A client requests deletion of their own review

6. Responding to Reviews

6.1 Practitioner Responses

Practitioners can respond to reviews:

  • Responses should be professional and constructive
  • Responses should not attack or insult the reviewer
  • Responses can offer clarification or their perspective on the feedback
  • Responses are public and visible to other clients

6.2 What NOT to Do in Responses

Practitioners should not:

  • Attack or insult the reviewer personally
  • Use responses as an opportunity for aggressive counter-arguments
  • Disclose private information about the client
  • Make false claims or lies in response
  • Use vulgar or abusive language
  • Pressure clients to change their review

6.3 Client Engagement

If a practitioner responds to your review:

  • You can edit your review if you want to address their response
  • You have the right to your opinion, whether or not they agree
  • You can ignore their response if you choose

7. Review Authenticity

7.1 Genuine Reviews Only

Reviews should be genuine and based on actual experiences:

  • Don't post fake reviews (positive or negative) for any reason
  • Don't pay for or accept payment for writing reviews
  • Don't write reviews pretending to be someone else
  • Don't coordinate with competitors to post negative reviews

7.2 What Happens if We Detect Fraud

If we identify fake or fraudulent reviews:

  • We will remove the fraudulent reviews
  • We may suspend or terminate the account responsible
  • We may ban the person from posting reviews in the future
  • We may pursue legal action if warranted

8. Review Disputes

8.1 If You Disagree with a Review

If you are a practitioner and disagree with a review:

  • You can respond publicly to the review (recommended)
  • You can request a review be removed IF it violates guidelines (not just if you disagree)
  • You can contact our support team if you believe the review contains false information
  • We will investigate if you believe the review violates our guidelines

8.2 If You Believe a Review Violates Guidelines

If you see a review that you believe violates our guidelines:

  • Report it through the review reporting feature
  • Provide details about which guideline(s) it violates
  • Our team will review and determine if action is needed
  • We appreciate community help in keeping reviews respectful

9. Privacy in Reviews

When posting a review, remember:

  • Your name is associated with your review (visible to others)
  • Don't share personal health details you want to keep private
  • Don't include the practitioner's private contact information
  • Don't name other clients or practitioners in your review
  • Consider what information you're comfortable sharing publicly

10. Review Ethics

10.1 For Clients Posting Reviews

We ask that you:

  • Be honest and fair in your assessment
  • Base your review on your actual experience
  • Be respectful even if critical
  • Give practitioners credit for what they do well
  • Remember that wellness outcomes vary from person to person
  • Consider that the practitioner is a real person reading your words

10.2 For Practitioners Reading Reviews

We ask that you:

  • Accept feedback gracefully, even negative feedback
  • Use reviews as an opportunity to learn and improve
  • Remember that different people have different needs and preferences
  • Not take negative reviews personally (not everyone is a good fit)
  • Respond professionally if you choose to respond
  • Never retaliate against clients for honest reviews

11. Special Circumstances

11.1 Reviews About Safety Concerns

If your review describes a safety concern or illegal activity:

  • Your review may stay on the platform
  • We may also report the concern to appropriate authorities
  • Your safety and the community's safety are our priority

11.2 Legal Disputes

If there's a legal dispute between a client and practitioner:

  • Reviews remain on the platform unless they violate guidelines
  • We don't remove reviews just because there's a legal disagreement
  • Both parties are welcome to share their perspective through reviews or responses

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Reviews Policy from time to time:

  • We will notify users of material changes
  • Your continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of updates
  • We will honor this policy for all reviews posted before changes
