Measuring Success: Your ROI Dashboard

Last updated: October 2025

Why ROI Matters

ROI (Return on Investment) is the single most important metric for pay-per-lead practitioners. It tells you whether Rooted Vitality is actually making you money—or costing you money.

Unlike a salaried job or flat monthly retainer, your income with pay-per-lead depends on conversion efficiency. Understanding your ROI helps you make data-driven decisions about where to invest your time and resources.

The Core ROI Formula

ROI = (Revenue from Conversions - Cost of Leads) / Cost of Leads × 100

Example:
• Cost per lead: $50
• Leads received this month: 20
• Total cost: $1,000
• Consultations booked: 10 (50% conversion)
• Average client revenue: $150 per session
• Total revenue: $1,500
ROI = ($1,500 - $1,000) / $1,000 × 100 = 50% ROI

A 50% ROI means for every dollar you spend on leads, you make $1.50 in return—a solid margin.

Key Metrics to Track

1. Cost Per Lead (CPL) What Rooted Vitality charges you per lead. Fixed and transparent. Example: $50/lead.
2. Conversion Rate Percentage of leads that book a consultation. Formula: (Consultations Booked / Leads Received) × 100. Target: 20-40% for experienced practitioners.
3. Average Client Revenue Average income per client consultation. Includes initial consultations and follow-ups. Example: $150 per session.
4. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Total revenue from a lead that converts to a long-term client. Example: If a lead converts and becomes a repeat client with 5 sessions at $150 each = $750 CLV.
5. Break-Even Point Number of leads needed before you start making profit. Formula: Total Monthly Expenses / Average Revenue Per Converted Lead.

Building Your Tracking System

Simple Spreadsheet Method

Use a basic spreadsheet to track:

  • Date lead received
  • Lead contact info (anonymized if needed)
  • Date contacted / Follow-up dates
  • Outcome (Booked / Not Interested / Pending)
  • Revenue generated (if converted)

Update weekly and review monthly. This gives you real-time visibility into what's working.

Monthly ROI Calculation

At the end of each month, calculate:

  1. Total leads received × cost per lead = Total Lead Cost
  2. Total revenue from all converted leads = Total Revenue
  3. Subtract lead costs from revenue = Net Profit
  4. Divide net profit by lead costs, multiply by 100 = ROI %

Healthy ROI Benchmarks

What should you aim for?

  • 0-25% ROI: You're breaking even or slightly profitable. Consider optimizing your conversion or rates.
  • 25-50% ROI: Good! You're making solid profit. This is sustainable.
  • 50-100% ROI: Excellent. You're converting well and commanding strong rates.
  • 100%+ ROI: Outstanding. You've mastered lead conversion or serve high-value clients.
Tip: Compare your PPL ROI to what you'd earn through traditional marketing (ads, website) or word-of-mouth. PPL often outperforms because leads are pre-qualified and low-risk for you.

Improving Your ROI

Increase Conversion Rate

Improve follow-up, strengthen your profile, get testimonials, and refine your pitch. Even a 5% increase in conversion rate significantly improves ROI.

Increase Average Revenue Per Client

Offer package deals (e.g., 5 sessions for discounted rate), upsell complementary services, or raise consultation rates slightly if demand is strong.

Reduce Cost Per Lead

Negotiate volume discounts with Rooted Vitality if you're consistently converting well and ready to scale.

Focus on Repeat Clients

A lead that converts to a 5-session client has 5x the lifetime value of a one-time consultation. Build strong relationships to encourage repeat bookings.

When to Scale

Once you're consistently achieving 25%+ ROI and have capacity for more clients, consider requesting additional leads. Track your metrics closely during scaling—more leads doesn't always mean better overall ROI if your conversion rate drops.

Scale gradually. Increase by 10-20% each month and monitor your conversion rate. If it holds steady, you've found a scalable model.

Final Thought: Numbers don't lie. Your ROI tells you whether pay-per-lead is working for your practice. Track it consistently, optimize continuously, and adjust your strategy based on data, not assumptions.
