How to Book a Consultation

7 min read • Patient Guide

In This Article You'll Learn

  • How to create your Rooted Vitality account
  • How to complete your wellness profile for better matches
  • How our matching system works
  • What to look for when reviewing practitioner profiles
  • The simple booking process and what happens next

Booking your first consultation with a holistic practitioner has never been easier. Rooted Vitality streamlines the entire process, connecting you with practitioners who align with your wellness goals in just a few simple steps.

The entire process is designed to be intuitive and personalized—we help you find the right practitioner match so you can focus on your wellness journey.

Step 1: Create Your Account

Start by creating your Rooted Vitality account. This takes just a few minutes and allows us to learn about your wellness goals, health interests, and preferences. Your account is secure and private—only you and the practitioners you choose to connect with will see your information.

During account creation, you'll share:

  • Your basic information and contact details
  • Your wellness goals and areas of interest
  • Your preferred modalities (herbal support, energy work, etc.)
  • Your schedule availability
  • Any specific health or wellness topics you'd like support with

Step 2: Complete Your Wellness Profile

Next, fill out your wellness profile form. This is where you provide more detailed information about what you're seeking. Think of this as a conversation starter—the more we know about your needs, the better matches we can suggest.

Your profile includes:

  • Current Wellness Status: How are you feeling now? What brought you to seek support?
  • Wellness Goals: What would success look like for you?
  • Preferred Modalities: Are there specific types of practitioners or healing approaches you're interested in?
  • Scheduling Preferences: Do you prefer in-person, virtual, or flexible sessions?
  • Additional Notes: Anything else a practitioner should know about you?

Step 3: We Match You With Practitioners

Once your profile is complete, our matching system reviews your information and suggests practitioners whose expertise, experience, and approach align with your wellness goals. We consider factors like:

  • Your stated wellness interests and goals
  • Practitioner specializations and modalities
  • Schedule compatibility
  • Preferred meeting format (in-person or virtual)
  • Your location or service area

You'll receive personalized recommendations tailored specifically to your needs.

Step 4: Review and Choose Your Practitioner

Browse the recommended practitioners at your own pace. Each practitioner profile includes:

  • Their background, certifications, and experience
  • Their approach to wellness and healing
  • Areas of specialization
  • Rates and session formats
  • Availability and scheduling options
  • Client testimonials (where available)

You have complete control—you can explore multiple practitioners, read their full profiles, and choose who feels like the best fit for you. There's no obligation to book with the first match suggested.

Step 5: Book Your Consultation

Once you've found a practitioner you'd like to connect with, booking is simple. Click to schedule your first consultation, choose your preferred time slot, and confirm your appointment. You'll receive:

  • Confirmation of your appointment details
  • The practitioner's contact information
  • Any pre-appointment information or preparation tips
  • Clear instructions for virtual sessions (if applicable)

What Happens Next?

Your practitioner will reach out to you before your first appointment to answer any questions and ensure you're comfortable. Your first consultation is an opportunity to:

  • Meet your practitioner and build rapport
  • Discuss your wellness goals in detail
  • Learn about their approach and methods
  • Ask any questions you have
  • Decide if you'd like to continue working together

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the booking process take?

The entire process typically takes 10-15 minutes. You can create your account, complete your profile, review practitioners, and book your first consultation all in one session.

Can I change my appointment after booking?

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel appointments directly through your Rooted Vitality account. Most practitioners offer flexibility for rescheduling. We recommend contacting them as soon as possible if you need to make changes.

What if I don't find the right practitioner match?

You're not limited to recommended matches. You can browse all available practitioners on the platform. You can also refine your profile preferences, and our system will generate new recommendations.

Is my information kept private?

Absolutely. Your account information is secure and private. We only share details with practitioners you choose to book with. We never sell or share your information with third parties.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment methods vary by practitioner. During booking, you'll see all available payment options for your chosen practitioner. Most accept credit cards, some offer payment plans.
